# The chat comes from the official extension

**Launcher for Claude Code** has no chat of its own. Conversations, sign-in, models and permissions belong to Anthropic's **Claude Code** extension.

The launcher adds:

- a shortcut to open the chat in the sidebar or in editor tabs;
- the list of the project's conversations, so you can resume any of them;
- a review of what Claude changed in each file;
- tabs for skills, rules, agents and MCP servers.

Nothing is installed until you click **Install Claude Code**.
