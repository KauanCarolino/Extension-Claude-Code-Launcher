# Review and decide

Every file Claude edited shows up in **Changes**, with the lines that came in and went out and what Claude said before editing.

- **In the editor**: markers show each hunk, with **Accept** and **Reject**. `Alt+]` and `Alt+[` move between hunks (on macOS, `Ctrl+Alt+]` and `Ctrl+Alt+[`).
- **In the panel**: accept or reject a whole file, or undo everything one request changed.
- **Reject** takes the file back to what it was before Claude. Right after, **Undo reject** brings the change back.

With a conversation in focus, the panel shows only its changes.
